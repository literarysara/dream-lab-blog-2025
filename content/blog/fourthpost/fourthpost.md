Today we sat down and go straight to work on our own websites. Just heads down, typing, and troubleshooting as needed. 

I was able to arrange the text and structure the way I wanted: overviews on every page, an accurate About page, no empty pages or data fields. I consolidated the two Timeline pages. I experimented with adding colorful markers on my map, with no success; my dataset is arranged in such a way that the subject field confounds my command to vary the colors, and it would not recognized another cleaner field. So that is something to keep working on.

I would have liked to have started exploring ways to deploy stream data in Leaflet, but I felt that I needed more time to practice and explore in Collection Builder using the site I already set up. And it took so much time! We were all editing and debugging right up until the moment we left our classroom for the showcase, except for a moment when we wrote up our takeaways on a giant notepad to share during the showcase. 

Highlights of the showcase included a group who used makerspace tools to create a board game about pollinators, a group called Creative Coding who created interactive storytelling tools, and a few different groups who worked on text analysis and visualization in various disciplines.
